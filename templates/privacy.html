Privacy Policy

How Spendly handles your information

1. Data We Collect

When you create an account we collect your name and email address. As you use Spendly, we store the expenses you log, including amounts, categories, dates and descriptions. We do not collect payment card or bank account details.

2. How We Use Your Data

Your data is used to run the service: to sign you in, to record your expenses, and to show you summaries and spending patterns. We do not sell your personal data or use it for advertising.

3. Data Storage

Your data is stored securely and kept only as long as your account is active. Passwords are stored in hashed form and are never kept in plain text. If you delete your account, your data is removed.

4. Third Party Services

Spendly loads fonts from Google Fonts, which may receive your IP address when a page loads. We do not share your expense data with any third party, except where required by law.

5. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or want your data deleted, please get in touch with us at support@spendly.example.
